# The check-in decided in code — registered before the runs

**Written 2026-09-05, built and gated, before any run.**

## Why

Same principle as the chart, same measured reason. Across the re-baseline the honest check-in was
**3 and 4 of 47**, and **24 of 94** turns closed on a clarifying OFFER, the one shape the contract
bans. Three belts and a length control failed to move it. The queue advances only on a turn that
taught — a visual **and** an accepted check — so a missing check meant "continue" re-taught the same
concept, which is exactly what the seat read found: outcome 4 failed with `taught: (none)` after two
turns.

## What it does

When a teach turn closes without a check `gradeCheckIn` accepts, and a chart is on screen, the
product appends one built from that chart's own links:

> *"Looking at the picture: if bigram_counts.py changed what it returns, which of nn_bigram.py or
> sampling.py do you think would break first?"*

It fires **only** when the model produced no accepted check, it is **appended, never substituted**,
and it is a **prediction** rather than "does that make sense?" because `docs/teach-mode.md` names
grading a prediction as the surviving differentiator and a comprehension check is answerable "yes"
by someone who followed nothing.

## Why this is not fabrication, which is the obvious objection

The chart had to be refusable because it **claims something about the repository**. The question
claims nothing: it names two files and a dependency direction that are already on the screen and
asks the learner to predict a consequence. It cannot be wrong about the code — it can only be a bad
question, which is a different risk, judged by reading it.

The direction is load-bearing and tested: asked about a file, the thing that BREAKS is what points
AT it, not what it points at. Getting that backwards would teach the dependency the wrong way round
while reading perfectly fluently.

## The prediction

Two runs, against the re-baseline's **check-in 3 and 4 of 47** (substantive 3/23 and 4/25):

- **check-in on the substantive denominator rises above 4 of 47 on both runs.** Near-mechanical:
  the derived chart already fires on most turns, and every turn with a chart and no accepted check
  now gets one.
- **outcome 4 on the seat run turns YES** — a second turn on the same thread advances instead of
  re-teaching.

**Refuted if** check-in does not clear 4 on both runs (the append is not reaching the turns that
need it), or if outcome 4 stays NO (something other than the missing check is holding the queue).

**And a report obligation rather than a metric:** if the derived check reads as a QUIZ rather than a
check-in, say so. The grader's clarifying/comprehension/prediction classes are the instrument for
the shape, but they cannot tell whether a learner would experience it as being tested rather than
taught, and that judgement belongs in the report in words.
