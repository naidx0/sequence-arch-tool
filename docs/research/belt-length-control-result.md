## Against what I registered: indeterminate, and I asked the wrong question

> **Predicted:** chart calls stay at 0 on both runs. **Refuted if:** they rise above 0 on both.

Chart calls were **0 and 2**. The prediction is not met — one run rose. The refutation condition is
not met either — only one run rose. By the replication rule, one run inside the compared range and
one outside is **no evidence**, in those words, and that is the verdict on the registered claim.

**The prediction was pointed at the wrong baseline, and that is my error rather than the run's.**
The control exists to ask *"is length sufficient to produce the full belt's tool use?"* — a question
about the **full belt's** range. I registered it against **zero**, which asks the different and
weaker question of whether padding does anything at all. A control compared to the wrong side
answers a question nobody had.

## Against the comparison the control was built for: clean, and it decides

Full belt, four runs: chart calls **[5, 9]**. Padded belt, same 7,219 characters of prompt, two
runs: **0 and 2**. **Both outside, both below, direction consistent.** That clears the replication
rule.

**Length is not the variable.** Six thousand characters of inert prose at exactly the full belt's
size produced 0 and 2 chart calls where the full belt's own six thousand characters produce 5 to 9.
So the cause lives in the CONTENT of the text the mid belt drops, and the next arm is a
**bisection of those ~6,000 characters** — halve it, two runs, keep the half that carries the calls
— not another rewrite.

## The one thing worth flagging as unresolved

`pad-2` produced **1/21 on the substantive visual denominator** — the first non-zero substantive
visual in the entire night, across fourteen runs. `pad-1` produced 0/21. One run of a pair, one turn
of twenty-one: by the rule that is **no evidence**, and it is recorded here only so that a later run
seeing the same thing knows it has been seen before rather than treating it as new.

## What this does not change

`4fc7ff9d` stands: 182 of 188 turns attempt no visual in any form. The control explains why no belt
edit has fixed that — the lever is in content nobody has isolated — but it does not make the
deterministic visual less necessary. A bisection would tell us WHICH sentence buys a chart call on a
model that still, at its best, drew on 1 turn in 21.
