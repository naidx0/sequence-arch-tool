An LLM, broken down

Four parts, and only the last one is the part people argue about:

  • Tokeniser — text in, integer ids out. Deterministic, boring, and the source of half your off-by-one bugs.
  • Embedding + attention stack — each token looks at every earlier token and rewrites itself. This is the whole trick.
  • Decoder head — one probability per vocabulary entry, sampled under a temperature.
  • Harness — tools, memory, approval. packages/analyzer is ours, and it is where grounding happens.

On the composer: it can hold the lock, but it does not today. Reading the call sites now.
