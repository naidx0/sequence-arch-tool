## Arm A — the ladder — refuted, and its reversal condition fired

- **check-in rises above 4 on both** → **3 and 2.** One inside the range, one below it, direction
  wrong. Refuted.
- **stubs fall below 22 on both** → **31 and 34.** Both outside, in the opposite direction, by a
  wide margin. Refuted — and this is the condition I registered as the one that would condemn the
  change: *"the ladder buys focus by spending rounds that used to buy prose."*

**The mechanism is legible in the word counts.** Median words per turn fell from 33 and 58 to 22 and
21. Told exactly one thing to fix, the model fixes that one thing and stops. Chart calls rose (5–7 →
9) and accepted charts rose (4–5 → 7) — it *did* obey the visual rung — but it delivered nothing
else around it.

So the wall diluted and the ladder tunnelled. Both fail, in opposite directions, which is
information: the problem was never how many complaints were shown. A turn that satisfies one rule
and abandons the rest is not closer to a lesson than a turn that satisfies none.

**Visual on substantive turns is 0 in every arm**, so the chart-call gain bought nothing on the
denominator that matters.

## Arm B — the mid belt — refuted, decisively

- **visual on substantive turns leaves 0 of 23 on both** → **0 and 0.** Refuted.
- **chart calls stay at or above [5, 7]** → **0 and 0.** Refuted, at the floor.

**This is the result that ends the belt line of inquiry.** The mid belt contains **both chart
bullets, byte-identical to the full belt** — a test asserts it — and produced **zero chart calls on
both runs**, exactly reproducing the short belt that was missing one of them.

| belt | chars | chart calls |
|---|---|---|
| full | 7,219 | 4, 12, 5, 7, **9, 9** |
| **mid (both bullets)** | **1,181** | **0, 0** |
| short (one bullet) | 654 | 0, 0 |

The arm's premise was that `:1057` — the bullet saying what a chart is FOR and what it is instead of
— is load-bearing for tool use. **It is not.** Restoring it verbatim changed nothing. Whatever makes
this model reach for `propose_chart` is in the ~6,000 characters the mid belt drops, or is a
property of belt length itself, and neither is the thing three arms have been testing.

## What the record now carries

Three belts have been tested — long, short, mid — and none moved the visual on a teaching turn off
zero. Set beside `4fc7ff9d`, where **182 of 188 turns attempt no visual in any form** — no tool call,
no wrong tool, no wrong format, no refusal — the conclusion is not that some other wording is
waiting to be found. **The contract is not the variable.**

The only two changes that moved a metric all night were a **tool redirect** and a **validator
message that named the admissible set** — both of them things the model reads *after* it acts,
not instructions it reads before.

## The one prediction of the night that held

Registered before these runs: *"I expect A to fail and B to fail."* Both failed. It is worth as much
as it cost — which is nothing, because the runs happened anyway — and it is the only forecast I got
right today, against three that I got wrong.

## Recommendation, not a decision

The ladder is an owner ruling and its reversal is not mine to take. The measurement says what it
says: **stubs 22–24 → 31–34, median words halved, check-in no better, and the chart-call gain
lands on turns that teach nothing.** By the criteria registered before the runs, that is the
condition under which the change should come out. I have left it in and am reporting it rather than
reverting a ruling on my own reading of my own numbers.
