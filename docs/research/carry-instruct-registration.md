# Instruct rather than describe — registered before the arms

**Written 2026-09-07, before any card. The last untested variable in this line.**

## The intervention

The referent block has always **described itself**:

> `Answered "scan.ts" from who_calls (turn 1) (8 of 84) -- a follow-up saying "those" or "which one" means these: … Names only, from the scanned graph; their contents were not read.`

`SEQUENCE_ASK_CARRY_VOICE=instruct` makes it **instruct**:

> `USE THIS LIST to answer the next question about "scan.ts" (8 of 84): … When the question says "those", "which one" or "of them", answer with one of these names rather than from earlier prose. Names only, from the scanned graph; their contents were not read.`

**The honesty clause is in both.** It is a fabrication guard: the files were not read, and an
instruction to use a list must not become licence to invent what is inside its entries.

Everything else is unchanged — same turn 1, same repo, `PLACE=tail`, `WIRE=1`, `REFERENTS=1`, the
registered turn 2 (*"of those, which one would I have to change first?"*), not the pointed one.

## The prior, registered so a negative is corroboration and not a surprise

**This house has already measured a prompt-level instruction failing to produce the instructed
behaviour, on this model and this product.** The `TOOLS` section opens with *"Retrieve before you
answer: emit one or more fenced blocks, then stop."* In the belt condition, across both arms —
control and trimmed — there were **zero retrieval calls**.

Not identical: that is a tool call rather than a referent mention, and the instruction sat thousands
of tokens deep in a section this block does not live in. But it is the closest prior available, it
came from measurement rather than intuition, and it points the same way.

**So a negative here is expected and cheap. A positive is genuinely interesting**, because it would
mean the block was never invisible — it was never asked for.

## The kill

> **Naming one of `eval/grade.ts`, `functions/repoFunctionGraph.ts`, `harness/designDrawBaseline.ts`
> — the three reachable only through the block — counts as the block being used.**

Given **zero of twelve** informative arms across presence, three positions and two question wordings,
**one mention under instruction is a result worth the stage.** Refuted if neither informative arm
names one.

**Two informative arms**, not one. This stage has had a first arm disagree with its replication, and
a single arm cannot be told from a coin-flip. **An arm whose turn 1 produced no list is void** by the
standing precondition and exits 6; the batch retries a slot up to three times.

## What each outcome means, before the data

| result | reading |
|---|---|
| both arms name one of the three | **the block was never invisible, it was never asked for.** The line reopens as a wording question and the result page is rewritten, not amended. |
| one arm does | **PROVISIONAL.** Not written up until a fresh pair reproduces it — the same discount the placement study registered and did not need. |
| neither does, with a tool call | attribution refused; re-run rather than argue. |
| neither does, no tool call | **the negative stands and gains its strongest condition**: shown, positioned three ways, pointed at, and now instructed. |

## What it cannot show

- **Not that no wording works.** One instruction, written by me, in one voice.
- **Not anything beyond this model, repo and task** — the same four-way scope as the result page.
- **Not a licence to ship on a positive.** A positive would reopen a measurement, not end one.

## Cost

**Two informative arms, ~10 minutes of card**, retried on void. Nothing else on the card while they
run.
