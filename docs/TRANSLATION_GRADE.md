## Weak spots & not-yet-proven

- **Word→system is mock-only (biggest caveat).** Axis 4 proves the normalization/plumbing is
  safe, deterministic, and capped — it does **not** prove a live model returns a coherent
  system for an English sentence. Treat Axis 4 as "the guardrails hold", not "the feature is good".
- **Thin-signal fixtures.** plainapp, shared-backend, dockerfile-env-mini, k8s-mini/helm, k8s-mini/plain are minimal fixtures whose
  classifier verdict rests on graph **shape** (≥2 services ⇒ microservices; 1 service + datastore
  ⇒ server-web), not a rich domain signal. The verdict is *defensible* but low-information; a real
  2-service app and a genuine microservice mesh currently read the same. Distinct per-type
  geometries are deferred to v10.
- **Ground truth is one repo.** Edge precision/recall is measured only on shopfront. The other
  repos have no hand-written edge truth, so their fidelity score rests on node-coverage only —
  a dropped/duplicated *edge* would not be caught outside shopfront.
- **Structural (no-AI) path only.** These grades use `buildStructuralTree` (the deterministic
  fallback). The AI grouping/relabel path is guarded by the honesty filter (invented refs
  stripped) but its *label quality* is not graded here — same live-model caveat as Axis 4.
- **`mobile-flutter` has 0 edges.** Its shape is bare (1 service, no interactions); the mobile
  verdict comes from framework signals, and there is essentially no topology to translate.

## Honest overall read

Where Sequence is genuinely strong is the **deterministic core**: code→structure fidelity (9/10) and plain-English labeling (9/10) are high because the structural translation drops nothing, invents nothing, and every label traces to a real source. The classifier (8/10) is accurate against our table but leans on graph shape for the minimal fixtures, so its real-world discrimination is softer than the number suggests. The honest ceiling is Axis 4 (7/10): the design-suggest **plumbing** is safe, but its **live-model coherence is unproven here** — that is the single biggest gap between this grade and a claim that the end-to-end word→system feature is "good". Overall, treat this as: *the parts that are deterministic are trustworthy; the parts that need a real model are only proven safe, not proven good.* (unweighted mean ≈ 8.3/10, but the per-axis numbers matter more than the average.)
